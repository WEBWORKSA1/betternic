End-User Outreach Email Generator

Write outbound sales emails to businesses that could use your domain. Pick a buyer type and angle; get a short, compliant pitch and follow-ups.

Outbound sales — contacting businesses that would benefit from your domain — is how many investors sell names that never get inbound inquiries. The best pitches are short, relevant to the buyer, and make the next step easy. This generator writes a first message and two follow-ups tailored to the buyer type and angle you choose.

How to use the End-User Outreach Email Generator

  1. Enter your domain, price (optional) and the buyer's company or industry.
  2. Choose an angle: brand upgrade, keyword match, competitor protection or expansion.
  3. Press Write pitch and personalise before sending.

How it works

Templates follow proven structure: specific subject line, one-line relevance, one benefit, a soft call to action, and an opt-out line. Keep outbound legal: follow anti-spam laws such as CAN-SPAM and Canada's CASL, identify yourself, and stop when asked.

When to use it

  • Selling keyword domains to local businesses
  • Pitching a .com upgrade to a company using another extension
  • Reaching companies whose names match your domain

Pro tips

  • Find buyers with the same keyword in their current domain, or companies running ads on the keyword.
  • Ten personalised emails beat 500 generic ones.
  • Mention the price range early to filter out non-buyers.

Frequently asked questions

Is cold emailing legal?

It depends on the country. Canada's CASL is strict about commercial email without consent; the US CAN-SPAM Act allows it with conditions. Check the rules for your recipients.

Should I include a price?

A price or range filters out non-buyers and often speeds up the conversation.
