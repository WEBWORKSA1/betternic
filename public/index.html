Frequently asked questions

Is BetterNic free?

Yes. All 37 tools, the daily auction feed, news and the Daily Brief are free. The site is supported by advertising, sponsorships and a commission only when a listed domain sells.

How is the data updated?

An automated pipeline runs every day: it downloads public auction inventory, industry news feeds, new videos and IANA's root zone and registration-server lists, scores everything and rebuilds the site.

What does it cost to sell a domain?

Listing is free. Commission is 5% under $50,000, 4% from $50,000 and 3% from $100,000.

Can BetterNic help me buy a domain someone else owns?

Yes. Use the acquisition request and a broker will contact the owner confidentially.
