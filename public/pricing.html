Ten ways to get paid

  • BetterNic-managed — We coordinate payment and escrow end to end. Easiest for first-time sellers.
  • Escrow.com — Industry-standard escrow opened directly between buyer and seller.
  • Sedo escrow / transfer service — For sellers who already use Sedo's transfer service.
  • Afternic — Seller arranges the transaction through their Afternic account.
  • Atom.com — Seller arranges the transaction through their Atom account.
  • Efty — Seller arranges the transaction through their Efty setup.
  • Stripe (seller's payment link) — Buyer pays the seller's own Stripe payment link.
  • PayPal — Best for smaller deals; PayPal payments can be reversed, so transfer after funds clear.
  • Wire via BetterNic — Buyer wires funds; BetterNic releases them to the seller after the transfer is confirmed.
  • Custom arrangement — Seller describes their own payment instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Are there any listing or subscription fees?

No. All 37 tools are free and listing is free. Commission applies only when a domain sells.

Is the tier marginal like income tax?

No. The rate for the tier your full sale price falls into applies to the whole price. A $60,000 sale pays 4% of $60,000.

Who pays escrow fees?

Buyer and seller agree this. Splitting 50/50 is common.

Do buyers pay a fee to BetterNic?

No buyer fee is charged by BetterNic. Buyers pay their own escrow or payment-method fees if agreed.
