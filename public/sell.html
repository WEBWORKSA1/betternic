Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to list?

Nothing. BetterNic charges a commission only when your domain sells: 5% under $50,000, 4% from $50,000 to $99,999 and 3% from $100,000, applied to the full sale price.

Can I list a domain that is also on other marketplaces?

Yes. BetterNic does not require exclusivity. Just keep your price consistent and remove listings promptly once it sells.

Which payment methods can I accept?

BetterNic-managed escrow, Escrow.com, Sedo, Afternic, Atom, Efty, your own Stripe link, PayPal, wire via BetterNic or a custom arrangement you describe.

How is the commission collected if I use my own escrow?

Where your escrow provider supports it, we set up a disbursement split. Otherwise the commission is invoiced and due within 7 days of the buyer's payment clearing.

Do you verify ownership?

Yes. Before publishing we ask you to confirm ownership, typically with a DNS TXT record or a registrar account check.
